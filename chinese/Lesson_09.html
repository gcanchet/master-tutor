Lesson 09: Ordering Food & Drinks

0. Review

Review: Wǒ zài fànguǎn (I'm at a restaurant). Zài nǎr? (Where?).

1. Vocabulary

#ChinesePinyinEnglish
1吃chīTo eat
2喝hēTo drink
3米饭mǐfànCooked Rice
4水shuǐWater
5想xiǎngWant / Would like

2. Core Sentence Patterns

  • Willingness: [Subject] + 想 + [Verb] + [Object]
  • Wǒ xiǎng chī mǐfàn. (I want to eat rice.)
  • Wǒ xiǎng hē shuǐ. (I want to drink water.)

Common Pitfalls

The "Xiǎng" Bug: There are many ways to say "want" in Chinese. 想 (xiǎng) is a polite "would like." It's great for ordering food in a civilized manner!

Practical Dialogue: At the Restaurant

Waiter: Nǐ xiǎng chī shénme? (What would you like to eat?)

Customer: Wǒ xiǎng chī mǐfàn, xièxie. (I'd like to eat rice, thanks.)
