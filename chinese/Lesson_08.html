Lesson 08: Locations (在)

0. Review

Review: Zhōngguó rén (Chinese person). Měiguó (USA).

1. Vocabulary

#ChinesePinyinEnglish
1在zàiTo be at / in
2哪儿nǎrWhere
3学校xuéxiàoSchool
4饭馆fànguǎnRestaurant
5这儿 / 那儿zhèr / nàrHere / There

2. Core Sentence Patterns

  • Question: [Subject] + 在哪儿? -> Lǎoshī zài nǎr? (Where is the teacher?)
  • Answer: [Subject] + 在 + [Location]. -> Wǒ zài xuéxiào. (I am at school.)

Common Pitfalls

The "Zài vs Shì" Bug: Use 在 (zài) for physical location. Use 是 (shì) for identity. Never say "Wǒ shì xuéxiào" unless you are literally a building.

Practical Dialogue: Finding a Friend

A: Nǐ zài nǎr? (Where are you?)

B: Wǒ zài fànguǎn. (I'm at a restaurant.)
