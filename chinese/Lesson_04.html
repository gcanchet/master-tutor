Lesson 04: Numbers 1-99

0. Review

Review: Use 吗 (ma) at the end of a statement to make it a question. Use 什么 (shénme) to ask "what".

1. Vocabulary (1-10)

2. Core Sentence Patterns

Logic: 11 is "Ten One" (shí yī). 20 is "Two Ten" (èr shí). 25 is "Two Ten Five" (èr shí wǔ).

  • 21 = 二十一 (èr shí yī)
  • 99 = 九十九 (jiǔ shí jiǔ)

Common Pitfalls

The "4 vs 10" Bug: Sì (4) and shí (10) sound very similar. Pay close attention to the tones: 4 is falling (4th), 10 is rising (2nd).

Practical Dialogue: Counting

A: Nǐ yǒu jǐ ge píngguǒ? (How many apples do you have?)

B: Wǒ yǒu sān shí liù ge. (I have 36.)
