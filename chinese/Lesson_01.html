Learn basic ways to greet people and ask how they are.

Core Sentence Patterns

Basic Greeting: Subject + 好

  • 你好。 (Hello.)
  • 老师好。 (Hello, teacher.)
  • 您好。 (Hello - polite.)

Practical Dialogue

A: 你好吗？ (How are you?)

B: 我很好，谢谢！ (I am very well, thanks!)

Common Pitfalls

The "Am" Bug: In Chinese, you don't use "to be" (shì) with adjectives. Do NOT say 我是很好. Simply say 我很好.
