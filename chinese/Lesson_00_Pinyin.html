Lesson 00: The Pinyin Environment

Before writing characters, we must master Pinyin—the system for transcribing Chinese sounds into the Roman alphabet.

1. The Four Tones

Mandarin is a tonal language. The same sound with a different tone has a different meaning.

Tap a character to hear the tone.

CharacterTone DescriptionPinyin (Blurred)
妈1st Tone: Flat/Highmā
麻2nd Tone: Risingmá
马3rd Tone: Falling-Risingmǎ
骂4th Tone: Falling/Sharpmà

2. Basic Initials (Consonants)

  • b, p, m, f
  • d, t, n, l
  • g, k, h

3. Basic Finals (Vowels)

  • a, o, e, i, u, ü

4. Common Pitfalls (The Tone Bug)

The "Flat Tone" Error: English speakers often drop the tone at the end of a sentence. In Chinese, you must maintain the tone's shape regardless of where it is in the sentence, or you might say "mother" (妈) when you mean "horse" (马)!

5. Practice

Try to identify the tones in these words:

  1. 你好 (Nǐ hǎo)
  2. 老师 (Lǎoshī)
  3. 谢谢 (Xièxie)
