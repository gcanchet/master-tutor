Chinese Language Learning Syllabus

Level 1: Foundation (Pinyin & Greetings)

  • Prerequisite: Lesson 00: Pinyin & Tones
  • Grammar: The 4 Tones, Basic Sentence Structure (S-V-O), The Question Particle "吗" (ma).
  • Vocab: Basic greetings, pronouns, numbers 1-10.

Level 2: Identity & Family

  • Grammar: Verbs "是" (shì) and "有" (yǒu), Negation with "不" (bù) and "没" (méi).
  • Vocab: Nationalities, professions, family members.

Level 3: Daily Life

  • Grammar: Time expressions, measure words, expressing likes/dislikes.
  • Vocab: Food, activities, dates and times.
