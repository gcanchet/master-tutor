Lesson 06: Family & Possession (的)

0. Review

Review: Monday is xīngqī yī. Tomorrow is míngtiān.

1. Vocabulary

#ChinesePinyinEnglish
1的de[Possessive Particle]
2爸爸bàbaDad
3妈妈māmaMom
4家jiāHome / Family
5有yǒuTo have
6没有méiyǒuTo not have

2. Core Sentence Patterns

  • Possession: [Person] + 的 + [Noun] -> Wǒ de jiā (My home).
  • Having: [Subject] + 有 + [Object] -> Wǒ yǒu bàba. (I have a dad.)

Common Pitfalls

The "No" Bug: To say "don't have," you must use 没有 (méiyǒu). You cannot say "bù yǒu." Yǒu is special and only takes méi.

Practical Dialogue: My Family

A: Zhè shì nǐ de māma ma? (Is this your mom?)

B: Shì, wǒ yǒu māma hé bàba. (Yes, I have a mom and a dad.)
