Lesson 07: Nationality

0. Review

Review: "I don't have" is méiyǒu. "My dad" is wǒ de bàba.

1. Vocabulary

#ChinesePinyinEnglish
1哪nǎWhich
2国guóCountry
3人rénPerson
4中国ZhōngguóChina
5美国MěiguóUSA

2. Core Sentence Patterns

  • Question: 你是哪国人? (Which country's person are you? / Where are you from?)
  • Answer: 我是 [Country] 人. -> Wǒ shì Zhōngguó rén. (I am Chinese.)

Common Pitfalls

The "Country" Bug: Unlike English where we change the word (China -> Chinese), in Mandarin you simply add 人 (rén) to the country name.

Practical Dialogue: Meeting a Foreigner

A: Nǐ shì nǎ guó rén? (Where are you from?)

B: Wǒ shì Měiguó rén. Nǐ ne? (I'm American. And you?)

A: Wǒ shì Zhōngguó rén. (I am Chinese.)
