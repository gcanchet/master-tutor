2. Core Sentence Patterns

Pattern: [Subject] + 是 (shì) + [Noun]

  • Wǒ shì lǎoshī. (I am a teacher.)
  • Tā shì xuésheng. (He/She is a student.)

Common Pitfalls

The "Shì" Bug: In Chinese, 是 (shì) is ONLY used to link two nouns. Do not use it with adjectives (like "I am happy").

Practical Dialogue: Introduction

A: Nǐ hǎo, wǒ shì Zhāng lǎoshī. (Hello, I am Teacher Zhang.)

B: Nǐ hǎo, wǒ shì xuésheng. (Hello, I am a student.)
