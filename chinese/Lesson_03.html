Lesson 03: Basic Questions

0. Review

Review: Wǒ shì xuésheng (I am a student). Note that 是 doesn't change for plural or different people.

1. Vocabulary

#ChinesePinyinEnglish
1吗ma[Question Particle]
2什么shénmeWhat
3名字míngziName
4叫jiàoTo be called

2. Core Sentence Patterns

  • Yes/No: [Statement] + 吗 (ma)? -> Nǐ shì lǎoshī ma? (Are you a teacher?)
  • What: [Subject] + 叫什么名字? -> Nǐ jiào shénme míngzi? (What is your name?)

Common Pitfalls

Word Order Bug: Chinese questions don't flip the verb. They keep the same order as a statement, but replace the unknown part with a question word or add ma at the end.

Practical Dialogue: Meeting Someone

A: Nǐ hǎo! Nǐ jiào shénme míngzi? (Hello! What is your name?)

B: Wǒ jiào Lǐ Huá. Nǐ shì xuésheng ma? (My name is Li Hua. Are you a student?)

A: Shì, wǒ shì xuésheng. (Yes, I am a student.)
