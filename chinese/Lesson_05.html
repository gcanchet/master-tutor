Lesson 05: Days & Weeks

0. Review

Review: Number 42 is sì shí èr. Remember the math: (4 x 10) + 2.

1. Vocabulary

#ChinesePinyinEnglish
1今天jīntiānToday
2明天míngtiānTomorrow
3星期xīngqīWeek
4几jǐHow many / which

2. Core Sentence Patterns

  • Days: 星期 + [Number] -> 星期一 (Monday), 星期二 (Tuesday).
  • Question: 今天星期几? (What day of the week is it today?)

Common Pitfalls

Sunday Bug: Sunday is not "Xīngqī qī". It is 星期天 (xīngqītiān) or 星期日 (xīngqīrì).

Practical Dialogue: Weekly Plans

A: Jīntiān xīngqī jǐ? (What day is it today?)

B: Jīntiān xīngqī sān. (Today is Wednesday.)
