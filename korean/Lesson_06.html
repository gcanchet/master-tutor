3. Grammar: Adjectives are Verbs

In Korean, adjectives are "descriptive verbs". They conjugate just like action verbs into the -아요/어요 form.

  • 학교가 커요. (The school is big.)
  • 이 책은 재미있어요. (This book is interesting.)

4. Common Pitfalls

The "Is" Redundancy: Do not use 입니다 with adjectives. 좋다 already means "is good". 좋다 입니다 is a logic error.

5. Practical Dialogue: Reviewing a Movie

A: 영화가 어때요? (How is the movie?)

B: 진짜 재미있어요! (It's really fun!)
