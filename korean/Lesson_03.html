3. Grammar: The Directional Particle (에)

Use 에 to indicate a destination with movement verbs like 가다 (go) and 오다 (come).

  • 집에 가요. (I go home.)
  • 학교에 와요. (I come to school.)

4. Common Pitfalls

The "In vs. To" Bug: In English, we say "go to" but "at home". In Korean, 에 covers both destination and static location (which we will see in Lesson 05).

5. Practical Dialogue: The Commute

A: 어디에 가요? (Where are you going?)

B: 회사에 가요. (I'm going to the office.)
