Lesson 09: Polite Requests (-(으)세요)

1. Review

Future plans: 공부할 거예요 (I will study). 쉴 거예요 (I will rest).

2. Vocabulary

3. Grammar: Polite Imperatives

Use this form to ask someone to do something politely.

  • 여기에 앉으세요. (Please sit here.)
  • 공부하세요. (Please study.)

4. Common Pitfalls

The "Social Hierarchy" Bug: Never use -세요 toward someone of much higher social status (like a CEO) if you are giving them a task. It's polite for daily life, but still a command.

5. Practical Dialogue: Ordering

A: 메뉴 좀 주세요. (Please give me the menu.)

B: 네, 여기 있어요. (Yes, here it is.)
