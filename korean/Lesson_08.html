Lesson 08: Future Intentions (-(으)ㄹ 거예요)

1. Review

Past tense check: 했어요 (did), 봤어요 (watched), 먹었어요 (ate).

2. Vocabulary

#KoreanPhoneticEnglish
1내일Nae-ilTomorrow
2주말Ju-malWeekend
3자다Ja-daTo sleep
4쉬다Swi-daTo rest
5여행하다Yeo-haeng-ha-daTo travel

3. Grammar: The Future/Intention Form

Add -ㄹ 거예요 to vowel stems, and -을 거예요 to consonant stems.

  • 갈 거예요. (I will go.)
  • 먹을 거예요. (I will eat.)

4. Common Pitfalls

The "Spacing" Bug: Note the space between ㄹ and 거예요. This is often missed in writing.

5. Practical Dialogue: Weekend Plans

A: 주말에 뭐 할 거예요? (What will you do on the weekend?)

B: 집에서 쉴 거예요. (I will rest at home.)
