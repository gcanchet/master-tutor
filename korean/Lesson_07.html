Lesson 07: Past Tense (~았/었어요)

1. Review

Adjective conjugation: 비싸다 -> 비싸요 (is expensive). 작다 -> 작아요 (is small).

2. Vocabulary (Time & Actions)

3. Grammar: Past Tense Formation

Insert -았- or -었- before the ending.

  • 가다 -> 갔어요 (Went)
  • 먹다 -> 먹었어요 (Ate)
  • 공부하다 -> 공부했어요 (Studied)

4. Common Pitfalls

The "Double S" Bug: Always look for the ㅆ bottom consonant. If you see 어요 without the ㅆ, it is present tense. 먹어요 (eat) vs 먹었어요 (ate).

5. Practical Dialogue: Yesterday's Recap

A: 어제 뭐 했어요? (What did you do yesterday?)

B: 친구를 만났어요. (I met a friend.)
