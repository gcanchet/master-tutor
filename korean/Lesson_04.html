Lesson 04: Numbers (Sino-Korean)

1. Review

Destination particle: Location + 에. Example: 식당에 가요.

2. Vocabulary (Sino-Korean 1-10)

Used for dates, money, minutes, and phone numbers.

3. Grammar: Stating Numbers

Combine number + counter + 입니다.

  • 삼 층입니다. (It is the 3rd floor.)
  • 오 번입니다. (It is number 5.)

4. Common Pitfalls

The "Number System" Bug: Korea uses two systems. Do not use these numbers to count people or hours (we will use Native Korean numbers for that later).

5. Practical Dialogue: Asking for a Floor

A: 사무실이 몇 층입니까? (What floor is the office?)

B: 팔 층입니다. (It is the 8th floor.)
