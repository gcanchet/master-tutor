Korean Language Learning Syllabus

Level 1: Foundation (Hangul & Identity)

  • Prerequisite: Lesson 00: Hangul
  • Grammar: Hangul Vowels/Consonants, Topic Particles (은/는), Terminal Copula (입니다).
  • Vocab: Basic greetings, self-introduction, Sino-Korean numbers.

Level 2: Actions & Objects

  • Grammar: Subject Particles (이/가), Object Particles (을/를), Present Tense (아/어/여요).
  • Vocab: Common verbs, food, family members.

Level 3: Time & Space

  • Grammar: Location Particles (에/에서), Past Tense (았/었/였어요), Pure Korean numbers.
  • Vocab: Dates, daily routines, locations.
