Lesson 05: Existence (있어요 / 없어요)

1. Review

Sino-Korean numbers: 1 (일), 2 (이), 3 (삼). Counter for floors: 층.

2. Vocabulary

#KoreanPhoneticEnglish
1있어요Is-seo-yoTo have / To exist
2없어요Eop-seo-yoTo not have / Not exist
3돈DonMoney
4시간Si-ganTime
5우산U-sanUmbrella

3. Grammar: Possession as Existence

In Korean, you don't "possess" an object as much as the object "exists to you". Use the subject marker 이/가.

  • 돈이 있어요. (I have money / Money exists.)
  • 시간이 없어요. (I don't have time / Time doesn't exist.)

4. Common Pitfalls

The Object Marker Bug: Beginners often say 돈을 있어요. This is incorrect. Always use 이/가 with 있어요/없어요.

5. Practical Dialogue: At the Store

A: 우산이 있어요? (Do you have an umbrella?)

B: 아니요, 없어요. (No, I don't.)
